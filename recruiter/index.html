Frequently Asked Questions
How does the 90-Day Candidate Ownership Lock work?
When you submit a candidate to an active mandate, an exclusive 90-day ownership lock is granted. No other recruiter can claim attribution or submit that candidate to the same company during this period.
When are placement commission payouts released?
Commission is cleared once the candidate successfully joins and completes the mandatory 90-day probation guarantee period agreed upon with the client.
What happens if the client already has my candidate in their database?
If the candidate was inactive or dormant for over 180 days, representation credit belongs to you for the new mandate. If actively engaged within 90 days, the submission is politely blocked.
